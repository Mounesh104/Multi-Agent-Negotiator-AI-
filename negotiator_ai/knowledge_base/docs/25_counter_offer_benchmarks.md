# Negotiation Counter-Offer Benchmarks by Category

## How to Use This Document
This document provides evidence-based counter-offer starting points by item category and leverage type. These are starting targets — your actual counter-offer should be calibrated to your specific deal facts.

**Important:** These are negotiation targets, not guaranteed outcomes. Actual results depend on supplier, market conditions, relationship, and deal specifics.

## Notes on Using These Benchmarks

### Always Adjust for:
1. **Your specific market conditions** — prices and discounts vary by region, supplier size, and current demand
2. **The supplier's own context** — a supplier with excess capacity will go lower; one at full capacity won't
3. **Your stated vs. actual leverage** — the benchmarks assume you can articulate your leverage clearly
4. **First-order "test" framing** — if you signal this is a test order with more to come, use the lower end

### The Importance of Market Research
These benchmarks are based on aggregated SME procurement data across industries. For your specific category and location, supplement with:
- Direct competing quotes (strongest evidence)
- Industry association pricing guides
- Online B2B marketplace pricing (IndiaMart, TradeIndia, Alibaba for comparable imports)
- Fellow business owner networks (informal market intelligence)

### When to Push Harder Than These Benchmarks
- Supplier has clear excess inventory or capacity
- Multiple competing quotes converge below these ranges
- Seasonal timing strongly in your favor
- You're offering an annual commitment with prepay

### When to Be More Conservative
- Single-source supplier (no real BATNA)
- Urgent need (supplier knows)
- Specialized/custom item (few alternatives)
- First-time buyer with no competing quotes

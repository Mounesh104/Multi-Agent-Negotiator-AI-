# Equipment Rental & Purchase Negotiation

## Equipment Rental vs. Purchase Decision
Before negotiating, clarify which is more advantageous:

| Factor | Rental Favors | Purchase Favors |
|---|---|---|
| Usage frequency | Occasional (<50% of time) | Regular (>50% of time) |
| Maintenance burden | Rental (included) | Purchase (manage yourself) |
| Technology obsolescence risk | Rental (upgrade easily) | Purchase (locked in) |
| Capital availability | Rental (low upfront) | Purchase (build asset) |
| Negotiation leverage | Lower (standard rates) | Higher (one-time buy) |

## Equipment Rental Negotiation Levers

### 1. Rental Period Commitment
Committing to a longer rental period (6–12 months vs. month-to-month) typically unlocks:
- 10–20% discount vs. monthly rate for 6-month commitment
- 15–25% discount vs. monthly rate for 12-month commitment
- Free delivery/pickup (worth ₹2,000–₹10,000 per trip for heavy equipment)

**Tactic:** "We're looking at a 6-month rental. What's your best rate for that commitment, and can you include delivery?"

### 2. Off-Peak Timing
Equipment rental rates vary significantly by season and demand:
- Construction equipment: cheapest in monsoon (lower demand)
- Event/AV equipment: cheapest on weekdays, January–February
- Cold storage: cheapest in winter months
- Office equipment (printers, etc.): relatively flat — negotiate on terms instead

**Tactic:** "We're flexible on start date by 2–3 weeks. If there's a period where your utilization is lower, we're happy to schedule then in exchange for a better rate."

### 3. Bundle Multiple Equipment Needs
If you need multiple pieces of equipment, bundle them in the same negotiation:
> "We need [Equipment A] and [Equipment B]. If we can agree on both today, what's the package rate?"

Multi-item bundles typically unlock 5–12% discount on the combination.

### 4. Negotiate Extras as Part of the Deal
Before closing on price, list all extras and negotiate them in:
- Delivery and pickup (both ways)
- Installation and setup
- Operator training
- Insurance/damage waiver
- Maintenance during rental period
- Fuel if applicable

"Include all of those in the rate, and we'll sign today."

### 5. Purchase-Option Clause
For longer rentals, negotiate a purchase-option clause:
> "If we're happy at the 3-month mark, we'd like the option to purchase the equipment with [X%] of rental fees credited toward the purchase price."

Rental-to-own arrangements with 50–70% of rental payments credited toward purchase are achievable with motivated sellers.

## Equipment Purchase Negotiation Levers

### New Equipment: Dealer Negotiation
- **End of quarter/year:** Dealers have sales quotas — last week of March, June, September, December are best times.
- **Floor models/display units:** 15–25% below list price; usually fully functional.
- **Last-year model:** When new model releases, negotiate 20–30% discount on the prior model.
- **Bundle service contract:** Negotiate AMC into the purchase price (add it as a condition, not an add-on).

### Used/Refurbished Equipment
- Target: 40–60% below new price for 2–4 year old equipment
- Negotiate: warranty (30–90 day minimum for anything >₹50,000)
- Require: demonstration before payment

## Benchmark Rates
- Month-to-month vs. 6-month commitment: **10–20% savings**
- Off-peak timing advantage: **5–15% below peak rates**
- Multi-item bundle discount: **5–12%**
- End-of-quarter new equipment discount: **5–15%**

# Payment Term Negotiation — Extended Terms (Net 30/60/90)

## Why Payment Terms Matter as a Negotiation Lever
Payment terms directly affect your working capital cycle. Extending payment terms from Net 30 to Net 60 is effectively a 30-day interest-free loan from your supplier. For a small business with $50,000/month in supplier spend, moving from Net 30 to Net 60 frees up $50,000 in working capital permanently.

## Standard Payment Term Norms (B2B, SME Context)

| Term | Description | Who Typically Qualifies |
|---|---|---|
| Net 15 | Payment due 15 days from invoice | New/unknown buyers; suppliers needing cash flow |
| Net 30 | Standard industry term | Most established SME relationships |
| Net 45 | Extended standard | Buyers with 1+ year relationship |
| Net 60 | Extended term | Higher-volume or longer-tenure buyers |
| Net 90 | Long-term extended | High-volume, high-trust relationships; large buyers |

## Early Payment Discount (2/10 Net 30)
"2/10 Net 30" means: take a 2% discount if you pay within 10 days; otherwise full amount due in 30 days.

**Annual equivalent of 2/10 Net 30:** ~36.7% annualized return on early payment (2% / 20 days × 365)
- This is an exceptionally high implicit interest rate. Only take it if you have idle cash.
- Negotiate for this option even if you don't plan to use it — it keeps flexibility.

## Negotiating Extended Terms

### Strategy 1: Tie Extended Terms to Volume
> "We're planning to grow our order volume by [X%] over the next quarter. To support that growth without straining our working capital, we'd need Net 60 terms. That helps us place larger orders more reliably."

### Strategy 2: Offer a Trade-off
If a supplier resists extending terms, offer something in return:
- **Auto-payment enrollment:** "We'll set up ACH auto-pay, so you get guaranteed on-time payment in exchange for Net 60."
- **Consistent order cadence:** "We'll commit to weekly/bi-weekly orders (more predictable for you) in exchange for extended terms."

### Strategy 3: Reference Industry Standards
> "Net 60 is standard for our industry with established supplier relationships. We've maintained perfect payment history with you for [X months] — we'd like our terms to reflect that reliability."

## Milestones-Based Payment Structure (for services/equipment)
For service contracts or equipment purchases, negotiate a milestone-based payment structure:
- **30% on contract signing**
- **40% on delivery/installation**
- **30% on satisfactory performance review (30 days post-delivery)**

This splits risk between buyer and supplier and is standard for service/equipment contracts >$10,000.

## Red Flags in Payment Terms
- **Prepayment requests >50%:** Acceptable for first orders; push back after establishing relationship.
- **No early payment discount offered:** Ask specifically — most suppliers will consider it.
- **Late payment penalties >1.5% per month:** Industry standard is 1–1.5%; push back on anything higher.

## Key Benchmarks
- Extended terms (Net 60) achievable after 6+ months of on-time payment history: **yes, in ~70% of supplier relationships**
- Early payment discount range: **1–3%** (2% is standard)
- Auto-pay discount (supplier-initiated): **0.5–1%** 

## Payment Term Conversation Opener
> "Before we finalize pricing, I'd like to discuss payment terms. We currently operate on Net 30 across most supplier relationships. As we grow this relationship, we'd like to move to Net 60 — which is standard for our order size and industry. What would make that work for you?"

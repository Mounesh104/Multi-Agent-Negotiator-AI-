# Service Contract & AMC Negotiation

## Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) — Overview
AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) negotiations are common for:
- Equipment (HVAC, refrigeration, machinery)
- IT infrastructure and software
- Facility maintenance (cleaning, security)
- Specialized equipment servicing

AMCs typically represent 8–15% of equipment purchase price annually. This is a significant recurring cost with substantial negotiation room.

## Key AMC Negotiation Levers

### 1. Multi-Year Commitment for Rate Lock
Offering a 2–3 year AMC commitment in exchange for a rate lock and upfront discount:
- 2-year AMC: **5–8% discount** vs. annual renewal
- 3-year AMC: **10–15% discount** + price escalation cap (typically CPI or 3% max annual increase)

**Tactic:** "We're prepared to sign a 3-year AMC. In exchange, we'd need the Year 1 rate to reflect the commitment value, and a cap on annual escalation at 3% or CPI, whichever is lower."

### 2. Scope Reduction Negotiation
AMC quotes often include services you don't need. Challenge the scope:
- **Exclude:** Services you can handle internally (basic filters, cleaning, minor adjustments)
- **Include explicitly:** What you can't do (specialized repairs, emergency callouts)
- **Benchmark:** Challenge costs line by line — labor rates, parts markup, callout fees

**Typical parts markup in AMC:** 15–40% above cost. Negotiate to cap markup at 20%.
**Labor rate benchmark:** Compare to market freelance rates for the same skills.

### 3. Response Time SLA with Penalty
> "We need guaranteed 4-hour response time for critical equipment failures with a penalty credit of [X%] of the monthly AMC fee per missed SLA. For non-critical issues, 24-hour response is acceptable."

This creates real accountability and filters out suppliers who cannot deliver.

### 4. Performance Milestone Payment
For new AMC relationships, negotiate milestone-based payments:
- 25% upfront
- 25% at 3-month service review
- 25% at 6-month review
- 25% at year-end renewal decision

### 5. Competitive Renewal Clause
> "We reserve the right to obtain competing quotes at renewal. We'll give you first right of refusal to match the best quote we receive."

This prevents complacency and creates ongoing competitive pressure at renewal.

## Common AMC Overpayment Traps
- **Unused emergency callouts bundled into base rate:** Pay per callout instead.
- **Parts at retail markup:** Negotiate supplier's cost + fixed markup (e.g., cost + 15%).
- **Auto-renewal without rate negotiation:** Always include a 60-day notice window for rate negotiation before auto-renewal.
- **Bundled insurance (equipment warranty extension):** Often priced at 2–3× market rate; get separate quote.

## Benchmark AMC Rates by Equipment Type
| Equipment Type | AMC as % of Equipment Value | Negotiation Target |
|---|---|---|
| HVAC/Refrigeration | 8–15% | 6–10% with multi-year |
| Industrial machinery | 5–10% | 4–7% with volume |
| IT/Servers | 15–20% | 10–15% competitive |
| Facility (cleaning) | Monthly flat rate | 10–15% discount for annual prepay |

## Service Contract Renewal Script
> "Our AMC is coming up for renewal. We've been satisfied with the service quality. However, we've received competing quotes in the [X–Y%] range. We'd like to continue with you, but we need the renewal rate to come in at [target rate]. What can you do for us as a long-term customer?"
